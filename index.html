Seva · सेवा

Small groups, real savings, lasting change

A self-help group is usually 10 to 20 people, often women from the same neighbourhood, who pool small savings and decide together how to use them. We help groups form, keep their books, and reach the next step.

Promote self-help groups

Guidance on forming a group, running meetings, keeping records and building trust between members.

Support weaker sections

Practical help for families who need it most, so that support turns into self-reliance.

  1. Form the groupMembers from similar circumstances come together and agree on rules, a meeting day and a saving amount.
  2. Save regularlyEvery member deposits a small fixed amount at each meeting. The record book is the group's memory.
  3. Lend within the groupPooled savings are lent to members for urgent needs, repaid with a small interest that stays with the group.
  4. Link with a bankA group with a steady track record can open a bank account and qualify for credit through SHG–bank linkage.
  5. Build livelihoodsWith credit and confidence, members start or expand small enterprises and support one another as they grow.

The four Mahāvākyas

प्रज्ञानं ब्रह्म

Prajñānaṃ brahma. Consciousness is Brahman. Aitareya Upaniṣad, Ṛgveda

अहं ब्रह्मास्मि

Ahaṃ brahmāsmi. I am Brahman. Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 1.4.10, Yajurveda

तत्त्वमसि

Tat tvam asi. You are That. Chāndogya Upaniṣad 6.8.7, Sāmaveda

अयमात्मा ब्रह्म

Ayam ātmā brahma. This Self is Brahman. Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad 2, Atharvaveda

ब्रह्म सत्यं जगन्मिथ्या जीवो ब्रह्मैव नापरः

brahma satyaṃ jagan mithyā jīvo brahmaiva nāparaḥ

Brahman alone is real, the world is appearance, and the individual self is not other than Brahman.

Attributed to Ādi Śaṅkarācārya
